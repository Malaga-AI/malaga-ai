Privacy Policy — Malaga-AI LinkedIn Analytics App

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains how the Malaga-AI community (“Malaga-AI”, “we”, “us”) handles data through its LinkedIn application (the “App”). The App is an internal tool. We use it only to collect and review analytics for the Malaga-AI LinkedIn page, so that we can understand how our community content performs and plan better events and posts.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is the Malaga-AI community, Málaga, Spain.
Contact: hello@malaga-ai.community
Website: https://malaga-ai.community/

2. Who uses the App

Only authorized Malaga-AI organizers who administer the Malaga-AI LinkedIn page sign in to the App. The App is not offered to the public, and it does not post content or send messages on anyone's behalf.

3. What data we collect

Through the LinkedIn API, and only with the permissions an organizer grants, the App may access:

  • Organizer account data: the basic profile of the signed-in organizer (name, LinkedIn member ID, and email address) and the OAuth access token LinkedIn issues. We use these only to authenticate the organizer and to confirm page admin rights.
  • Page analytics: aggregate statistics for the Malaga-AI LinkedIn page, such as follower counts, page views, impressions, clicks, reactions, comments, shares, and engagement rates.
  • Follower demographics: aggregate, anonymized breakdowns that LinkedIn provides (for example by location, industry, seniority, or function). These do not identify individual people.
  • Post data: the content and performance metrics of posts published by the Malaga-AI page.

We do not collect the private profiles, connections, or messages of LinkedIn members who follow or interact with our page.

4. How we use the data

  • To measure the reach and engagement of Malaga-AI posts and events.
  • To produce internal reports.
  • To improve the content and timing of our community communications.

We do not use the data for advertising, profiling of individuals, or automated decision-making.

5. Legal basis

For organizer account data, we rely on the organizer's consent, given when they authorize the App on LinkedIn. For aggregate page analytics, we rely on our legitimate interest in understanding how our community page performs (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade any data. We do not share data from the App with third parties, except with service providers that host our infrastructure (bound by data-processing terms), or when the law requires it.

7. Storage and retention

  • We store access tokens securely and only for as long as they are valid or until access is revoked.
  • We keep aggregate analytics for up to 24 months, then delete them or keep them only in anonymized summary form.
  • We comply with the LinkedIn API Terms of Use, including its data-storage and deletion requirements.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access to authorized organizers, and secure storage of credentials.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. You can withdraw consent at any time by removing the App in your LinkedIn settings (Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Permitted services) or by emailing hello@malaga-ai.community. You may also file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will show the date of the latest change at the top of this page.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, contact us at hello@malaga-ai.community.
